SPICY BICOL EXPRESS

Description

Bicol Express is a spicy Filipino dish made with pork cooked in coconut milk, shrimp paste, chili peppers, garlic, onion, and ginger. It is creamy, salty, rich, and spicy. It comes from the Bicol region, which is known for dishes with coconut milk and chili. It is usually served with steamed rice.

Ingredients

  • 1 kilogram pork belly
  • 2 cups coconut milk
  • 2 tablespoon shrimp paste
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 thum-sized ginger, sliced
  • 3-5 chilli peppers, sliced
  • 2 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 cup water
  • Salt
  • Peppe

Steps to Cook Mouth Watering Pork Humba

  1. Cut 1 kilogram of pork belly into serving-size pieces. Heat a pot over medium-high heat and brown the pork in its own fat until lightly golden. This gives the humba a richer flavor.
  2. Add 1 chopped onion and 5 cloves of minced garlic. Cook for 2–3 minutes, until fragrant.
  3. Pour in ½ cup soy sauce, ¼ cup vinegar, ½ cup brown sugar, and 2 cups water. Add 2 bay leaves and 1 teaspoon whole peppercorns. Do not stir immediately after adding the vinegar; let it boil first for about 2 minutes.
  4. Lower the heat, cover the pot, and simmer for 1 to 1½ hours. Stir occasionally and add a little water if the sauce becomes too dry. Cook until the pork is very tender.
  5. Add 1 small can of pineapple chunks with a little of its juice, ½ cup banana blossoms, and ¼ cup salted black beans. Simmer for another 15–20 minutes.
  6. Taste the sauce. Add more brown sugar for sweetness, soy sauce for saltiness, or a small amount of vinegar for more tang.
  7. Continue cooking uncovered for a few minutes until the sauce becomes thick, glossy, and coats the pork. Serve hot with steamed rice.
