SPICY BICOL EXPRESS

Description

Bicol Express is a spicy Filipino dish made with pork cooked in coconut milk, shrimp paste, chili peppers, garlic, onion, and ginger. It is creamy, salty, rich, and spicy. It comes from the Bicol region, which is known for dishes with coconut milk and chili. It is usually served with steamed rice.

Ingredients

  • 1 kilogram pork belly
  • 2 cups coconut milk
  • 2 tablespoon shrimp paste
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 thum-sized ginger, sliced
  • 3-5 chilli peppers, sliced
  • 2 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 cup water
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Steps to Spicy Bicol Express

  1. Sauté the garlic, onion, and ginger until the onion is soft and fragrant.
  2. Add the pork belly cubes and cook until lightly browned and some fat renders out.
  3. Stir in the shrimp paste and half of the chopped Thai chilies.
  4. Cook for about 2 minutes.Pour in the coconut milk (gata).
  5. Bring it to a boil, then lower the heat to a gentle simmer.
  6. Cover and cook for 30 to 40 minutes until the pork is tender.
  7. Add the sliced finger chilies (siling haba) and pour in the thick coconut cream (kakang gata).
  8. Simmer uncovered until the sauce thickens and the oil starts to render from the coconut cream.
  9. Season with salt and pepper to taste, then serve hot over steamed rice
