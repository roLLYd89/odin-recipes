CHICKEN AFRITADA

Description

CHICKEN AFRITADA Chicken afritada is a Filipino chicken stew cooked in tomato sauce with potatoes, carrots, bell peppers, peas, and sometimes hotdogs. It tastes savory, slightly sweet, and tomato-rich. It is usually served with steamed

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Bay Leaves
  • Potato
  • Carrot
  • Hotdog
  • Green Peas
  • Red Onion
  • Garlic
  • Chicken Broth
  • Brown Sugar
  • Cooking Oil
  • Salt
  • Ground Black Pepper

Steps to Cook Chicken Afritada

  1. Heat the oil in a cooking pot, saute the onion and garlic until the onion softens.
  2. Add the chicken and cook for 30-40 seconds, then turn each piece over and cook the other side for another 30 seconds until golden brown.
  3. Pour in the tomato sauce and chicken broth, cover the pot, and let it boil.
  4. Add the dried bay leaves, lower the heat, and cook covered for 30 minutes, remember adding more broth or water when it starts to dry out.
  5. Add the carrot, along with the hotdogs when you are using them, and cook for 3 minutes.
  6. Add the potato, cover, and cook for 8 minutes, then stir in the green peas and cook for 2 more minutes.
  7. Season with salt and ground black pepper to taste, or use fish sauce in place of the salt.
  8. Give everything a gentle stir, then serve hot with rice.
